About

I’m Eugene Yakhnenko, a software engineer with over ten years in the industry, currently at Virtru, where I work on frontend platforms, developer tooling, and platform standards. I also contribute to OpenTDF. I care as much about the person using what I build, whether an end user or another engineer, as about the architecture underneath.

You can download my resume.

Outside of work, I build open-source tools: TTT (a terminal IDE), Autentico (an OIDC identity provider), and kasper-js (a reactive framework). When I’m not writing code, I’m into photography, plants, and oil painting.
